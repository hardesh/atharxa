Balanced stones — variations

Stones are now hand-shaped (flat undersides, slight offsets) instead of perfect ellipses, so the stack feels natural. Each variant: Android circle mask, macOS rounded square, 48 dp.

B1 · Refined

Primary

The base mark: three stones in balance under a small saffron sun. Light ground, Forest greens.

B2 · Night

Dark

Same stones on deep forest, lit from within. Strong on dark home screens and the macOS dock.

B3 · Still water

Calm

The stack stands at the edge of still water with a soft reflection. The calmest version; the most “ataraxia”.

B4 · Spark

AI

The sun becomes the assistant’s sparkle, the same shape as the “Ask Atharxa” button. Ties the icon to the AI inside.

B5 · Goal ring

Progress

The stack sits inside a goal ring three-quarters closed, echoing the water ring and day progress in the app.

B6 · Themed (mono)

Android 13+

One-colour version for Android themed icons; the system tints it to the wallpaper. Gaps between stones keep it legible.
